The Game

A Card Game Generations Grew Up With

Kaachu Phool (also known as Kachuful or Judgement) is an iconic Indian trick-taking card game. The name is a Gujarati mnemonic for the strict cyclical rotation of trump suits — making every round a fresh battle of wits.

How to Play

Simple Rules, Deep Strategy

1

Bid Your Hand

Look at your cards and predict how many tricks you'll win. The Hook Rule prevents the dealer from making the total bids equal the cards dealt.

2

Play Your Cards

Follow suit, play trumps, and outsmart your opponents. Trump rotation follows Ka-Chu-Fu-L — each round the trump suit shifts.

3

Score & Repeat

Hit your bid for bonus points. Miss and you get zero (or negative in Penalty mode). The highest score after all rounds wins.
